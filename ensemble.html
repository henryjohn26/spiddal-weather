How to interpret this forecast

These are model-relative ensemble frequencies, not calibrated real-world probabilities. A 60% reading means 60% of the valid members in the selected model meet the threshold. Do not combine member counts across different models.

At least 10 valid members are required before a probability or range is displayed. A dash means insufficient members, unavailable data or an incomplete calendar day. Some models have shorter forecast horizons. Missing wind-gust data is not interpreted as calm weather.
